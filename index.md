### TrayReach 支持

感谢使用 TrayReach。如果你在使用过程中遇到问题，或希望提出功能建议，请通过以下方式联系我们。

**联系邮箱**  
secsupport@163.com

为了更快定位问题，建议在邮件中提供：

- macOS 版本；
- TrayReach 版本；
- 问题发生前执行的操作；
- 错误提示或截图；
- 是否可以稳定复现。

请勿发送私人文件、付款信息、Apple ID 密码或其他敏感内容。

**常见问题**

**TrayReach 是否需要账号？**  
不需要。TrayReach 不提供开发者账号系统。

**为什么需要选择文件夹？**  
macOS 沙盒要求用户明确授权 App 访问所选文件夹。TrayReach 使用系统文件选择器和安全作用域书签保存该授权。

**如何恢复购买？**  
打开 TrayReach Pro 页面，选择“恢复购买”。购买和订阅由 Apple App Store 处理。

**TrayReach 会上传文件吗？**  
不会。文件名、路径、文件内容、托盘配置和使用记录均不会上传到开发者服务器。

### TrayReach Support

Thank you for using TrayReach. If you encounter a problem or would like to suggest a feature, contact us at:

**Support email**  
secsupport@163.com

To help us investigate, please include:

- your macOS version;
- your TrayReach version;
- the steps performed before the issue occurred;
- the error message or a screenshot;
- whether the issue can be reproduced consistently.

Do not send private files, payment details, Apple Account passwords, or other sensitive information.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Does TrayReach require an account?**  
No. TrayReach does not operate a developer account system.

**Why does TrayReach ask me to choose a folder?**  
The macOS sandbox requires explicit permission before an app can access a folder. TrayReach uses the system file picker and security-scoped bookmarks to preserve this permission.

**How do I restore a purchase?**  
Open the TrayReach Pro page and choose Restore Purchases. Apple’s App Store processes purchases and subscriptions.

**Does TrayReach upload my files?**  
No. File names, paths, contents, tray configurations, and usage records are not uploaded to developer-operated servers.
