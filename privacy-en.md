# TrayReach Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how TrayReach handles information when providing desktop trays, application launching, file shortcuts, file collection, and file organization on macOS.

## 1. How we handle information

TrayReach does not require an account, display advertisements, or track users across apps. The app does not upload file names, file paths, file contents, tray settings, or usage records to developer-operated servers. It does not integrate third-party analytics, crash reporting, third-party sign-in, or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information on your device

To provide its features, TrayReach stores or reads the following information on your Mac:

- tray names, tabs, shortcuts, window positions, appearance settings, and opening preferences;
- names, locations, and icons of installed applications, to display and launch them;
- counts and timestamps of applications or files opened through TrayReach, for Recent and Frequent views;
- names, paths, types, modification times, and authorization bookmarks for files and folders you authorize, to display, search, and open them;
- records and original locations of collected or organized files, to show their source and support restoration;
- purchase entitlement information verified by Apple and a local cache, to determine access to Pro features.

The developer does not centrally store these local settings and records. Removing a shortcut, source, or tray configuration does not delete the corresponding application or actual files. Copying, moving, and restoring files are operations you initiate and can change where those files are stored.

## 3. File access and control

TrayReach uses the macOS sandbox and system authorization mechanisms to access files and folders you select or drag into the app. It stores security-scoped bookmarks for subsequent access. File searches are limited to tray contents and explicitly authorized locations; the app does not scan your entire disk for files.

Core features do not require Full Disk Access, Accessibility, or Automation permissions. You can remove shortcuts or trays you no longer need. If you need help managing local data, contact the email address below. The developer cannot remotely read or delete data on your Mac.

## 4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple’s App Store handles purchases, subscription payments, and refunds. TrayReach obtains and verifies purchase entitlements through Apple’s StoreKit and may cache entitlement status in the local Keychain. The developer does not receive or store card details, billing addresses, or other payment information.

Apple’s handling of this information is governed by its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Contacting support

If you email secsupport@163.com, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond, investigate issues, and handle your request. These emails are not data automatically uploaded by the app; they are processed and stored through email service providers.

Please provide only information needed to resolve the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary private files. You may request deletion of previously submitted support emails by contacting the same address. We will handle the request subject to what is needed to address it and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. External websites

When you open support pages, privacy policies, or Apple links, the website hosting provider may receive technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address and browser information. That information is handled under the relevant provider’s privacy policy, separately from TrayReach’s on-device file processing.

## 7. Policy updates

If features or information practices change, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page. Where additional authorization is required, we will explain it and obtain the appropriate authorization before enabling the relevant feature.
